Custom back LED effects for POCO

If you are searching for a custom notification LED, RGB notification ring, rear light effect or per-app LED color on a POCO X8 Pro, Aura controls the phone's back light while the screen is off. It works locally and does not require root when used with Shizuku.

The POCO X8 Pro Max, also known as Redmi Turbo 5 Max, shares the same RGB ring hardware and has been confirmed working by users.

Features

Per-app colors

Assign a default RGB color to each enabled app.

Per-sender rules

Use different colors and animations for contacts and groups in messaging apps.

Animations

Choose breathing, flashing, rainbow or alert effects.

No root required

Use Shizuku and ADB-level privileges to drive the LED rings.

FAQ

Can I customize the LEDs on a POCO X8 Pro?

Yes. Aura controls the back RGB LED rings and supports a color and animation for each app, contact or group notification.

Does Aura support HyperOS?

Yes. Aura is developed and tested for the POCO X8 Pro running HyperOS 3. Enable the phone's back light effects first, then disable HyperOS System LED in Aura to avoid double lighting.

Quels sont les termes de recherche en français ?

Aura permet la LED personnalisée du POCO X8 Pro, la lumière arrière POCO, l'anneau LED de notification HyperOS et la couleur LED par application POCO.
