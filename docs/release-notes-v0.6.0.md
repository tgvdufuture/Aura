# Aura 0.6.0

This is the first release that can tell you a new version is out and install it for you. It also adds optional anonymous statistics, and a line in Settings that says whether Aura came back on its own after your last reboot.

## Anonymous statistics, off until you say yes

- Aura can now share which features you use, how far setup got, your app and Android version and your device model — to prioritise what to build next.
- Nothing leaves your phone until you agree, the screen appears once, and it can be turned off again at any time in Settings.
- Never collected: notification content, contact names, message text.

## Updates from inside the app

- Aura checks for a new version once a day and installs it in one tap, from the dialog or from a quiet notification when the app is closed.

## Smaller things you will notice

- The mascot has a place in the update dialog, the privacy screen and the empty states, and release notes now read as text instead of raw markdown.
- Settings reports whether Aura came back by itself after the last reboot. HyperOS gives no app a way to read its autostart switch, so Aura reports what it observed rather than guessing at your setting.
- Back in Settings, and in the re-opened privacy screen, now returns where you came from instead of closing the app.
