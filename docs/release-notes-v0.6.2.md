# Aura 0.6.2

The in-app update now finishes: permission, download, install.

## If you are on 0.6.1

- Tapping Update sent you to a settings screen and left nothing to tap? Fixed. Allow "Install unknown apps" for Aura, come back: the Update button is back.
- Closing that dialog only declined 0.6.1. Aura offers this one on its own daily check.

## What changed

- **Update resumes.** Granting "Install unknown apps" no longer leaves the dialog stuck on "Open settings" with no download button.
- **A failed update says why**, in your language, and offers the releases page instead of stopping there.
- **A star link lives in Settings** now, not only in the update dialog, for everyone who never sees one.

## Unchanged

- No new permission, no new screen, no new setting, no new data. Your rules, colours, language and preferences are untouched.
- Same signing key as 0.6.1: the update installs in place.
