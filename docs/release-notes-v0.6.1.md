# Aura 0.6.1

This release tells you straight away when Aura cannot light your phone, instead of letting you set the whole thing up for nothing.

## Before you install

- Aura drives the rear RGB ring, which only the Xiaomi/HyperOS family exposes. The phones confirmed working are the **POCO X8 Pro** and the **POCO X8 Pro Max** (Redmi Turbo 5 Max).
- On any other phone, Aura now says so on its first screen and keeps a quiet line on the app list afterwards.

## Nothing is blocked

- The notice is information, not a gate: the app list, the rules, the settings and the LED test all keep working, and one tap on "Continue anyway" opens the app as usual. If your phone can in fact light up — a rebrand, a HyperOS port — Aura still lets you try, and the test result is what really tells you.
- No new permission, no new setting, no new data collected.

## Why it changed

- Of the first installs of 0.6.0 from outside the Xiaomi family, every LED test failed and not one light ever came on: five phones, twelve attempts.
- Those users were asked to install Shizuku and grant privileges for a phone that cannot work, and the app never said a word about it. Now it does, before the download and inside the app.

## If your phone is one of the two

- Nothing changes for you: no new screen, no new permission, no new setting, and your rules, colours and preferences are untouched.
